Gym Management System

Manage your complete gym operations with an end-to-end Gym Management System built for member enrollment, health assessments, workout & diet planning, attendance tracking, and renewal billing in one integrated Odoo platform. Seamlessly handle membership plans and automated invoicing across multiple branches, assign dedicated trainers and nutritionists to every member, and generate personalised workout & diet plans straight from a health assessment. The module also supports daily attendance tracking, automated renewal reminders, a real-time analytics dashboard, and a member self-service portal, helping your team run the gym smoothly from sign-up to renewal.

Key Features

Everything you need to manage member enrollment, health assessments, workout & diet planning, attendance, and renewal billing end to end

Membership Management
Create configurable membership plans and enroll members with automatic sequence numbering and full renewal history.
Health Assessments
Record BMI, body measurements, and fitness goals for every member, then assign a trainer and/or nutritionist to follow up.
Workout Plans & Templates
Build reusable weekly workout templates by fitness goal, then generate a personalised plan straight from a health assessment.
Diet & Nutrition Plans
Create meal-by-meal diet templates backed by a food & calorie database, and generate personalised nutrition plans for members.
Attendance Tracking
Check members in and out, track session duration automatically, and flag anyone still checked in after 12 hours.
Automated Billing & Renewal
Confirm memberships into Sales Orders and Invoices automatically, and renew expiring memberships in one click with a dedicated wizard.
Analytics Dashboard
Real-time KPIs for active memberships, revenue, attendance trends, and staff workload in one interactive dashboard.
Member Self-Service Portal
Members log in to view their membership, workout & diet plans, and attendance history online.
Trainer & Nutritionist Assignment
Assign dedicated trainers and nutritionists to each member and track their workload from the Employee record.
Role-Based Security & Multi-Branch
Dedicated Receptionist, Trainer, Nutritionist, Branch Manager, and Administrator roles, with data scoped per branch (company).
Automated Reminders
Daily scheduled actions automatically expire memberships past their end date and remind members before their membership expires.
Staff Attendance & Check-In
One-click Check In / Check Out wizard for receptionists, trainers, and nutritionists, with a dedicated Staff Attendance screen for admins.

Feature Deep Dive

Explore every capability of our Gym Management System in detail

  • Configuration
  • Release Notes
  • FAQ
01

Module Installation

Install the Gym Management module from Odoo Apps. Once activated, a dedicated Gym Management menu appears with Dashboard, Members, Fitness, Diet, Staff, Reporting, and Configuration.

02

Assign Gym User Roles

Go to Settings > Users and open the Access Rights tab. Enable the relevant Gym Role checkbox — Receptionist, Trainer, Nutritionist, Branch Manager, or Administrator — to control what each user can see and do in the app.

03

Membership Plan Configuration

Turn any product into a Membership Plan from its Membership Plan tab: set the plan code and duration, toggle whether it includes a workout and/or diet plan, and list the member benefits.

04

Workout Templates

Build reusable weekly workout templates per fitness goal. Each template's Exercise Lines define the day of week, exercise, category, equipment, sets, reps, and rest time.

05

Exercise Categories & Configuration Menu

The Configuration menu groups every master data list — Membership Plans, Workout Templates, Exercise Categories, Exercises, Diet Templates, Food Categories, Foods, Equipment Categories, Equipment, and Trainer Specializations — for quick setup.

06

Exercise Catalog

Maintain the Exercise catalog with each exercise's category, targeted muscle group, and required equipment. These exercises populate the workout template lines.

07

Diet Templates

Create meal-by-meal Diet Templates per fitness goal. Each template's Meal Lines define the meal type, food item, quantity, and unit, sourced from the food catalog.

08

Food Categories

Organize the food catalog into categories such as Carbohydrates, Protein, or Vegetables. Each category groups foods with their calorie and macronutrient values for easy reuse in diet templates.

09

Food Catalog

Maintain the Foods catalog with calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat per item. These nutritional values feed automatically into diet templates and member diet plans.

10

Equipment Categories

Group gym equipment into categories like Cardio Machines, Strength Machines, or Free Weights so it can be referenced consistently across exercises and workout templates.

11

Equipment Catalog

Maintain the full Equipment catalog, assigning each machine or accessory to its category so it's ready to attach to exercises and workout plans.

12

Trainer Specializations

Define Trainer Specializations such as CrossFit, Strength & Conditioning, or Weight Loss Coaching to categorize trainers by their area of expertise.

13

Staff Gym Role Assignment

On each Employee record's Gym Info tab, the Receptionist, Trainer, Nutritionist, and Branch Manager flags are read directly from that employee's linked user Access Rights, keeping staff roles in sync.

14

Staff Check In / Check Out

Staff use the Check In / Check Out wizard under Staff to log their own working hours in one click, built on top of Odoo's native hr.attendance model.

15

Staff Attendance Overview

The Staff Attendance list under Staff shows every employee's check-in and check-out times and worked hours, so admins can review attendance across all staff.

16

Member Registration & Portal Access

Mark a Contact as a Gym Member on its Gym Info tab, then click Create Portal User to give the member login access to the self-service portal.

17

Creating & Confirming a Membership

Create a Membership by selecting the member, plan, and start/end dates, then click Confirm & Invoice to generate the Sales Order and Invoice and move the membership toward Active.

18

Membership Activation & Welcome Email

Once the invoice is paid, the membership automatically activates and a welcome email is sent to the member. From here, click Create Assessment to schedule their initial health assessment.

19

Health Assessment – Body Measurements

Record the member's height, weight, BMI, body fat percentage, and other body measurements, then assign a Trainer and/or Nutritionist and click Assign & Notify to alert them.

20

Generating Workout & Diet Plans

From the in-progress assessment, pick a workout template under Workout Recommendation and a diet template under Diet Recommendation, then click Create Workout Plan or Create Diet Plan to generate the member's personalised schedule.

21

Activating the Workout Plan

The generated Workout Plan lists exercises by day of week with sets, reps, and rest time. Click Activate to publish it and move its status from Draft to Active.

22

Member Portal – My Account

Members log into the customer portal to reach My Account, with quick links to their Memberships, Workout Plans, Diet Plans, and Attendance.

23

Member Portal – Membership Details

Inside My Memberships, a member can see their plan status and dates, linked Workout Plans, Diet Plans, and Health Assessments, plus their recent attendance — and check in directly from the page.

24

Member Portal – Self Check-In / Check-Out

From the portal Attendance page, members check themselves in and out and see their complete visit history with check-in/out timestamps.

25

Admin Attendance Overview

The Members > Attendance list gives admins a consolidated view of every member's check-in/check-out records and linked workout plan.

26

Branch Manager Role & Multi-Branch Access

Assign the Gym Branch Manager role to a user and set their Companies and Default Company to scope their access to a specific branch.

27

Branch-Scoped Dashboard

When a Branch Manager logs in, the top-right company selector defaults to their assigned branch, and the Gym Overview dashboard shows figures for that branch only.

28

Switching Between Branches

Administrators with access to multiple branches can switch between them from the same top-right company menu to view consolidated or branch-specific gym data.

29

Analytics Dashboard – Members & Billing

The Members & Billing dashboard tab shows real-time KPIs for total and active members, new signups, expiring memberships, pending payments, and revenue, alongside membership and payment status charts.

30

Analytics Dashboard – Attendance & Staff

The Attendance & Staff dashboard tab tracks check-ins, members currently in the gym, average session length, peak hours, top trainers, and staff attendance in one interactive view.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I enroll a new member?
Mark a Contact as a Gym Member, create a Membership choosing a plan and an optional Trainer, then confirm it to generate the Sales Order and Invoice automatically.
Can members view their workout and diet plans online?
Yes. Members can log in to the portal to see their membership, workout plan, diet plan, and attendance history at any time.
How is attendance tracked?
Reception checks members in and out against their active membership, and session duration is computed automatically once they check out.
Can I assign different trainers and nutritionists per member?
Yes. Each membership can have its own assigned Trainer, and each health assessment can have its own Trainer and/or Nutritionist for workout and diet follow-up.
Does the module support multiple gym branches?
Yes. Branches are modeled as companies, and record rules scope memberships, plans, and attendance to the right branch for each user.
Does the module include an analytics dashboard?
Yes. The dashboard shows real-time KPIs for active memberships, revenue, attendance trends, and staff workload, all in one interactive view.
